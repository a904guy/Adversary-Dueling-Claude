
  You've selected the WORKER pane.

  That's fine for watching, copying output, or pressing Esc to stop it.

  To give instructions, use the ADVERSARY pane on the right. It passes
  them to the worker and keeps checking the work against them.

  If you do type a message here, press p in the bridge pane first to
  pause the relay, so a relayed message isn't typed into yours.

  This tip is shown once per project.
